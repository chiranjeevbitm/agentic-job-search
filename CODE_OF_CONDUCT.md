# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, assume good faith, give and accept
constructive feedback, and do not harass or discriminate. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits or contributors that violate these principles.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer via the email on their
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
